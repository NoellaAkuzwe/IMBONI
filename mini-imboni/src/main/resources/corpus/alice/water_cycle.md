# The Water Cycle

The water cycle describes how water moves continuously between the oceans, the air and the land. The Sun provides the energy that drives the cycle.

## Evaporation and transpiration

Heat from the Sun causes water in oceans, lakes and rivers to evaporate, turning from liquid into water vapour. Plants also release water vapour from their leaves in a process called transpiration.

## Condensation and precipitation

As water vapour rises, it cools and condenses into tiny droplets that form clouds. When the droplets join together and become too heavy, they fall as precipitation: rain, snow, sleet or hail.

## Collection

Precipitation collects in oceans, lakes and rivers, or soaks into the ground to become groundwater. Water that flows over the surface of the land back to rivers and the sea is called runoff. From there, the cycle begins again.
