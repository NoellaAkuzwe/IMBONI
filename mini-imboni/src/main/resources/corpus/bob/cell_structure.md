# Cell Structure

All living things are made of cells. Animal cells and plant cells share several structures, but plant cells have some extra parts.

## Structures in animal and plant cells

The nucleus contains genetic material and controls the activities of the cell. The cell membrane controls which substances enter and leave the cell. The cytoplasm is a jelly-like substance where most chemical reactions happen. Mitochondria are where aerobic respiration takes place, releasing energy from glucose.

## Extra structures in plant cells

Plant cells also have a cell wall made of cellulose, which strengthens the cell. They have a permanent vacuole filled with cell sap, which keeps the cell firm. Many plant cells contain chloroplasts, where photosynthesis takes place.

| Structure | Animal cell | Plant cell |
|---|---|---|
| Nucleus | Yes | Yes |
| Cell membrane | Yes | Yes |
| Cell wall | No | Yes |
| Chloroplasts | No | Yes |
| Permanent vacuole | No | Yes |
